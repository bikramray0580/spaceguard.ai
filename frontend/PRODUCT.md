# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mission analysts monitoring orbital objects and conjunction risk from a desktop or tablet workstation.

## Product Purpose

SpaceGuard makes the orbital environment legible at a glance, then exposes selected-object telemetry, risk, simulation state, and supporting intelligence when needed.

## Positioning

An orbital-awareness workstation that keeps the spatial environment—not a dense collection of widgets—as the primary working surface.

## Capabilities and Constraints

React/Vite web application with React Three Fiber orbital visualization, existing mock telemetry, selected-threat state, and simulation progress. Preserve current data and interaction behavior during visual restructuring.

## Brand Commitments

SpaceGuard is a calm, precise aerospace mission-control product. The locked dashboard reference establishes the dashboard's dark, restrained, glass-surface visual direction.

## Evidence on Hand

Existing orbital and mission data in `src/data/`, operational UI flows in `src/`, and the user-supplied locked visual reference.

## Product Principles

- Put the orbital environment first.
- Reveal operational information at the moment it is needed.
- Make risk unmistakable without making the workspace feel alarming.
